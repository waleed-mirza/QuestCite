# Incident Response Plan

> SAMPLE DATA — NOT LEGAL OR COMPLIANCE ADVICE.

This fictional plan supplies incident-response evidence for the Northstar Cloud demo questionnaire.

## Detection and triage

The response process defines detection, triage, severity, escalation, evidence preservation, communications, and post-incident review. Each incident receives a severity, an accountable owner, and a documented response timeline.

## Evidence and containment

Responders preserve relevant logs and evidence while avoiding exposure of customer content. Containment and recovery actions are recorded with the owner and follow-up work needed to close the event.

## Communications

The plan includes customer communication criteria and an approved escalation path. This fixture does not set a contractual maximum time to notify customers after a confirmed incident. That answer requires legal or customer-success review before export.

## Learning

Material incidents receive a post-incident review with root cause, actions, and assigned follow-up. The review output is handled as controlled internal material in the fictional workflow.
