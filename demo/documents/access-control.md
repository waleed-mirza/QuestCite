# Access Control Standard

> SAMPLE DATA — NOT LEGAL OR COMPLIANCE ADVICE.

This fictional standard describes the identity and access controls used by the Northstar Cloud sample workspace.

## Workforce access

Multi-factor authentication is required for workforce accounts and privileged access. Privileged operations use separate elevated access paths with stronger authentication and monitoring. Access is granted according to job responsibilities and the least-privilege standard.

Joiner, mover, and leaver procedures include prompt account disablement, removal of active privileged sessions where supported, and review of access changes. Privileged access is recertified quarterly and after material role changes.

## Administrative activity

Administrative actions are recorded in an append-only audit trail containing safe metadata such as action type, entity type, and timestamps. Document contents, prompts, and answer text are not included in application logs.

## Training evidence

Security responsibilities are assigned to control owners. This fixture does not include independent training-completion certification; an owner must confirm that question before an external export.
