# Data Retention Schedule

> SAMPLE DATA — NOT LEGAL OR COMPLIANCE ADVICE.

This fictional schedule demonstrates how a workspace can document retention and deletion controls.

## Retention

The schedule records periods for account data, audit metadata, questionnaires, and uploaded document metadata. Workspace owners and admins can choose a retention period within the configured bounds.

## Deletion and export

An owner can start a confirmed deletion workflow. The application records the request before a controlled purge. The owner-only export contains safe workspace metadata and does not include document contents or signed URLs.

## Residency review

Processing regions are documented for the sample environment. A dedicated data-residency option is not established in this fixture; the deployment region and contract terms require confirmation.
